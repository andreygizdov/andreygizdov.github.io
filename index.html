About

Hi. I do research on compute-efficient and cognitively inspired multimodal models. I've been fortunate to work with Tomer Ullman at Harvard, Boris Katz at MIT, and Daniel Harari at the Weizmann Institute. I dropped my PhD to start something new.

  • My dog
  • Sport and cold showers. I'd be nowhere without them.
